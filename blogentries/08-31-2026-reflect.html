reflect

I'm going to try and put out more "useful" blogs. I don't really like writing my thoughts just to write them down! I like keeping them to myself and mulling over them. It feels almost egotistical writing for anyone other than myself (like where it can be accessed by other people), when the content of what I'm writing about consists of only about myself, and my thoughts. So I'm changing how I do things. I want to give things to you, the reader. Or if it's me reading this, I want to give things to you, a place to keep a record of information that might be useful to you. It's probably easy to discern the meaning of what I mean by a "useful" blog. I want to write about things I've learned about, things I think I could explain well. Or maybe just provide referential works for myself, like recipes or tutorials, or notes, with my own experience included.


I think every time I've written something I've felt like I was trauma dumping or writing a manifesto. And like. Those are cool but I really don't want to read those, and I don't think anyone else would want to read them either. And I don't write here much anyway... My end goal was always using this website more, but I'm not going to use the website if the things on it aren't useful to me. I like my book log I have, and in theory the other logs that would be coming soon, since I like having those things all in one spot for myself. But the blogs... I don't know what I was doing those for, it was really just to do it. I would always end up writing when I was feeling a little neurotic, so I would ramble on for 4 paragraphs then apologize for rambling in the next. So if the end goal is using this website more, and writing more, I'm giving myself a reason to! I guess purely referential material isn't exactly a blog how I imagine a blog, but I don't intend on making things that are PURELY referential. (Mostly) Don't expect anything until it actually happens. Everything I say is subject to change. I'm sitting in a lecture hall right now, and it might be alterning my brain chemistry. I might be a different person by the time I get out of here.
